# Jason Keen

**Independent Researcher | Financial Intelligence, OSINT & Geopolitical Risk**

I build Python research tools that connect public-source evidence with financial and geopolitical risk analysis. My work includes sanctions and ownership research, energy-market exposure, scenario modeling, and local economic monitoring.

The projects below demonstrate how I organize evidence, make assumptions explicit, test analytical methods, and communicate uncertainty through reports and dashboards.

## Start here

- **[Sanctions Ownership Network Investigator](https://github.com/jasonrkeen/sanctions-ownership-network-investigator)** — Entity screening and ownership-network review with source records and retained analyst decisions. Start with the [fictional-case walkthrough](https://github.com/jasonrkeen/sanctions-ownership-network-investigator/blob/main/docs/DEMO_WALKTHROUGH.md). Screening outputs require human review and do not establish legal sanctions status.
- **[Energy Markets & Geopolitical Risk](https://github.com/jasonrkeen/energy-markets-geopolitical-risk-study)** — An empirical study of oil-price changes, geopolitical risk, and energy-equity returns. Read the [research report](https://github.com/jasonrkeen/energy-markets-geopolitical-risk-study/blob/main/docs/RELEASE_V2.md) and inspect the recorded reproduction checks. Findings are retrospective associations.
- **[Maritime Chokepoint Transmission](https://github.com/jasonrkeen/multi-commodity-maritime-chokepoint-transmission-model)** — Conditional scenarios tracing disruption across crude oil, LNG, fertilizer, and helium. Start with the [runnable example](https://github.com/jasonrkeen/multi-commodity-maritime-chokepoint-transmission-model#quick-start). Dollar outputs represent modeled gross exposure, not realized losses. See the [release and provenance boundary](https://github.com/jasonrkeen/multi-commodity-maritime-chokepoint-transmission-model#release-and-provenance).
- **[Financial Sector Risk Intelligence](https://github.com/jasonrkeen/financial-sector-risk-intelligence-model)** — Public-source jurisdiction indicators, scenario comparisons, and a Streamlit dashboard. View the [dashboard examples](https://github.com/jasonrkeen/financial-sector-risk-intelligence-model#screenshots). Scores are analyst-defined indicators for further review.

## How I work

- Separate observations, assumptions, proxies, and synthetic examples.
- Record source dates, configurations, and random seeds where applicable.
- Distinguish software checks and sensitivity analysis from empirical validation.
- Preserve methodological corrections and explain unresolved limitations.

Reproduction requirements and validation status vary by project. Each repository documents its scope and evidence.
